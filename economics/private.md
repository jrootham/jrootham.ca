# Private Landlords

## Summary and Recommendations

Private landlords are a bad idea because markets will only drive prices down to the costs of new construction, not the (much lower) costs of existing buildings.

This not only directly causes affordability issues but also creates a very large wealth transfer from renters to landlords. This is exactly the problem that Smith and Ricardo found objectional in their era.

The solution to this problem is enough non profit housing to remove the market power of private landlords. This housing does not need to be subsidized, it just needs to charge only enough to cover its costs. It does not matter if the housing is owned directly by the government, is a not for profit co-op, or is owned by a not for profit organization.
