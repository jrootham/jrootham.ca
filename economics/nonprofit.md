#### Not For Profit

A not for profit organization might use a rental property to generate income for its other purposes.  In this case they will use a for profit pricing strategy.

Not for profit owners have three possible strategies.

1. Fixed price with subsidies.
2. Rent geared to income with subsidies.
3. Prices set at costs.

These strategies can be mixed.  Co-ops typically use 2 and 3.
