### Charts

The charts are about cash flow, they do not include more complex calculations of profit and loss.

#### Graphs of nominal dollar flows

The nominal dollar charts are best interpreted as the history of a typical rental project.

Here is the first option: $2,900 rent and 25 year amortization period.

![Graph in nominal dollars - flat mortgage, operating costs increasing by inflation, replacement reserves increasing by more than inflation , total costs, rent increasing by inflation, margin is rent minus total costs](nominal25.svg)

Here is the second option: $2,600 rent and 50 year amortization period.

![Graph in nominal dollars - flat mortgage, operating costs increasing by inflation, replacement reserves increasing by more than inflation, total costs, rent increasing by inflation, margin is rent minus total costs](nominal50.svg)

#### Graphs of constant dollar flows

The constant dollar charts are best interpreted as the typical cash flow this year of a unit built that many years ago.

Here is the first option: $2,900 rent and 25 year amortization period.

![Graph in constant dollars - declining mortgage, flat operating, increasing replacement reserves, total costs, flat rent, margin is rent minus total costs](constant25.svg)
