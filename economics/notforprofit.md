# Existing Not for Profit

Older not for profit (most of them) have a political problem of requiring larger percentage increases as replacement reserves increasingly dominate their budgets.

This is most directly seen in co-ops, since political problems are transparent in democratic organizations.

It also shows up in government social housing (like Toronto Housing) since politicians tend to make decisions on short timelines.
